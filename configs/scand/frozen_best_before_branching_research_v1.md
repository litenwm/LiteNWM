# Frozen best baseline before branching research

The fallback incumbent remains formal-v70 and its three full ensemble
checkpoints. The highest held-route performance prototype remains the frozen
union65 listwise selector.

The selector improved mean objective by 5.33 mm and mean ATE by 2.53 mm over
formal-v70 on 800 spent development states, but its 41.25% empirical harm rate
and negative route-cluster bootstrap lower bound make it a performance
reference only. It is not a deployable or safety-certified replacement.

All referenced weights, contracts, predictions, and data are bound by SHA256
in `frozen_best_before_branching_research_v1.json`. Further research must write
to separate paths and must not open sealed validation.
